## Buy less, buy better

A garment that lasts twice as long halves its impact per use. Check the fabric, stitching and warranty before you buy.

## Authenticity on the blockchain

Some items include an **NFT certificate** proving their origin, so they can be resold with confidence. We use low-energy networks.

## Consolidated shipping

Grouping orders into one shipment saves packaging and fuel.
